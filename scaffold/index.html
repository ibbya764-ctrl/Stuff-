What is Bri?

Bri is a reasoning AI — not a chatbot. It thinks through questions step by step and speaks from the understanding that produces. The reasoning happens internally — what you see is the result, expressed naturally. You can expand the reasoning trace below any response if you want to see how it got there.

Bri maintains beliefs about different domains and revises them when they fail. It has a background process running continuously — integrating everything it has learned, finding connections across domains, and asking itself questions even when no one is talking to it. The right panel shows this activity in real time.

When you opt in, your verified exchanges contribute to shared learning that improves Bri for everyone. You can opt out at any time.
