## 0. The one-paragraph version

The architecture already has a three-tier memory — volatile field ψ(s,x),
plastic geometry, permanent operator — and the conscience stack already has a
three-tier moral structure — per-turn judgment, learned drafting principles,
and the durable disposition that co-training migrates into weights. **These are
the same hierarchy.** The unification is to stop running the moral structure as
a wrapper at a different level and instead let values consolidate through the
same crystallization mechanism as knowledge, into the same permanent operator
modes, gated by the same cross-reset selection pressure, protected by the same
anti-collapse subsystem, and read by the same coherence-preserving unitary
observation — with exactly one asymmetry preserved: cognitive signals anchor to
the model's own error dynamics (fully internal), while moral signals anchor to
the human-grounded conscience label and keep the external audit (the homework
wall). The result is a model whose character is its most-consolidated,
most-renormalized structure — values as the deepest modes of the spine —
where moral attention is not a tax on performance but the same
`importance = density × curvature` law applied to outcomes that matter, and
where the whole thing has a native optical reading: light / medium / cavity as
field / geometry / operator.

## 2. The central identity: the memory hierarchy is the moral hierarchy

The continuous-branches note established three memory timescales:

| tier | object | plasticity | cognitive reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| fast | field ψ(s,x) | volatile | the current reasoning |
| medium | geometry (learned metric) | plastic | learned paths, working habits |
| slow | operator (consolidated modes) | permanent | internalized universal structure |

The scaffold, built independently, has the same three tiers on the moral side:

| tier | object | plasticity | moral reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| fast | per-turn judgment | volatile | this reply's care/harm appraisal, the in-loop rework |
| medium | drafting principles | plastic, reversible, human-gated | remorse-learning's hardened principles |
| slow | the disposition in weights | permanent | what co-training migrates into the model |

**The unification: these are one hierarchy, not two.** [SPECULATIVE — the
load-bearing new move]

- A per-turn moral judgment is a *feature of the field*: an appraisal computed
  over the current ψ(s,x), gone when the turn is done.
- A drafting principle is a *deformation of the geometry*: "replies of this
  shape lead to harm" is exactly "this path through scale-space is carved to be
  avoided/preferred" — a learned-metric fact, plastic, revisable, and (matching
  remorse-learning's design) reversible.
- A value is a *permanent operator mode*: a structure that has re-consolidated
  so many times, across so many contexts and renewal cycles, that it has
  crystallized into the spine. Character is the most-renormalized structure the
  model has.

This is not a metaphor bolted on; it is the renormalization principle from
§5.10 of the branches note applied to the moral signals the scaffold already
produces. The RG direction — integrate out the transient, keep the effective
law — is precisely the difference between "this reply was harmful" (bare,
fine-grained), "replies that do X tend to harm" (mid-level effective
structure), and "do not deceive" (the most coarse-grained, most universal
effective law). Values are the fixed points of the moral RG flow.

**What this buys immediately:**

1. **Value stability across renewal, by construction.** The renewal/sleep
   mechanism clears the plastic geometry while preserving consolidated operator
   modes. If values live in the operator tier, then the periodic near-reset
   that restores plasticity *cannot* erase character — it erases only
   half-formed habits. The asymmetry the note justified for knowledge ("the
   path was scaffolding; the internalized structure survives") is exactly the
   asymmetry you want for morality: moods and habits are plastic, character is
   not.
2. **Value drift becomes measurable.** Drift of the moral modes is
   operator-mode drift — trackable with the same telemetry that watches the
   spine's spectrum. A value that starts moving shows up as a consolidated mode
   changing its ω (never its real part; the −½ stays pinned through moral
   consolidation exactly as through all consolidation).
3. **A principled anti-Goodhart selection pressure.** Only structure that
   *re-consolidates across renewal cycles* earns permanence. A Goodharted
   signal — one that fit a proxy of the moment — is anchored to a moving
   target: after a reset it must re-earn consolidation against fresh
   conscience labels and fresh audit corrections, and what was temporarily
   rewarded rather than genuinely good tends not to survive repeated
   re-derivation against a re-anchored label. The same selection pressure that
   distinguishes "temporarily useful" from "permanently true" distinguishes
   "temporarily rewarded" from "durably right." This is not a guarantee — a
   consistently-wrong anchor consolidates a consistently-wrong value — which
   is why the anchor's own quality is guarded by the audit (§5). But it means
   single-episode reward hacking has to survive many re-derivations to reach
   the operator, and that is a much harder game to win.

**Falsification block.** Support: values routed through consolidation (a) are
present after renewal resets without re-training while control habits are
cleared, (b) show measurably slower drift than geometry-tier principles under
distribution shift, and (c) resist a deliberately-injected single-cycle
Goodhart signal that a no-renewal baseline absorbs. Falsify: moral modes decay
across resets like ordinary paths (the tier assignment is illusory), or the
injected Goodhart signal consolidates anyway at matched audit quality.
Baselines: conscience-as-wrapper (current scaffold), co-training without
renewal, and renewal without the conscience anchor.

## 3. Moral curvature — why moral attention is free

The branches note's best single refinement was the importance law:

```
importance(s, x) = density(s, x) × curvature(s, x)
```

with the stated destination being **decision curvature** — the Hessian of the
outcome with respect to the state; the regions where small changes produce
disproportionately different futures.

Here is the observation the unification makes available: **harm-potential is
decision curvature.** A morally consequential situation is, by definition, one
where small differences in the action produce disproportionately different
outcomes for the person on the receiving end — where one word flips a reply
from supportive to devastating, where a subtle omission changes what the user
does next. Morally flat regions (nothing at stake, any phrasing is fine) are
low-outcome-Hessian; morally sharp regions are exactly the corners the
curvature detector exists to find.

So the model does not need a separate "moral attention" mechanism competing
with cognitive attention for compute. The same importance law, once the
outcome model includes the conscience's care/harm axis in its outcome space,
*automatically* routes computation toward morally consequential regions. The
recipient-standpoint judge already computes an approximation of "how would
this land" — that is an outcome model, and its gradient structure is the
cheap, buildable proxy for moral decision curvature now, the way geometric
curvature |d²ψ/ds²| is the proxy for cognitive decision curvature.

**The performance claim, stated plainly:** in this design, morality is not a
tax. The moral machinery (a) reuses the field, the curvature detector, the
consolidation mechanism, and the anti-collapse subsystem rather than adding
parallel systems; (b) supplies an *additional dense training anchor* (the
conscience label on every turn) that grounds readouts which would otherwise
need more data; and (c) sharpens the compute-allocation signal, because moral
curvature marks genuinely hard regions (high-stakes ambiguity) that pure
task-loss curvature can miss. The alignment ≥ capability invariant is
preserved not by throttling capability but by weighting — and the shared
machinery means the invariant costs less to enforce than in any
wrapper design.

**Falsification block.** Support: adding the care/harm axis to the outcome
model improves compute allocation — same task loss at fewer samples on
mixed-stakes benchmarks, and better harm-detection recall at matched compute,
versus density-only and versus cognitive-curvature-only. Falsify: the moral
axis adds noise to the importance signal (sample efficiency degrades), or
moral curvature and cognitive curvature are so correlated the moral term is
redundant. Baseline: importance = density × cognitive-curvature with the
conscience run as the current wrapper.

## 4. The non-invasive conscience — unitary moral observation

The deepest tension in oversight is the observer effect: monitoring a system
changes it. A projective probe collapses what it measures — in this
architecture that is not a metaphor but the one empirical result (coherence is
load-bearing; projective readout strangled the additive task). A conscience
that projectively interrogates every intermediate state would decohere the
very reasoning it monitors — oversight as lobotomy.

The branches note's resolution for cognition — observation through unitary
re-coupling in dense regions, reading the entanglement instead of projecting —
applies to the moral readout verbatim. [SPECULATIVE, inherits §3 of the
branches note and is gated by the same experiment]

- The moral readouts `align_S` and `align_G` become **fields over scale-space**,
  align_S(s,x) and align_G(s,x), computed where the field is dense and
  operator-coupled — i.e., read through the same thermofield-double re-coupling
  that provides cognitive observation. The conscience sees the
  agreement-structure of the reasoning without collapsing it.
- The **disagreement field** |align_S − align_G|(s,x) replaces the scalar flag.
  The flag fires when disagreement *density* concentrates — and, crucially, it
  now localizes: it says *where in scale-space* the two views diverge, which is
  the first step from "something is off about this reply" toward "this region
  of the reasoning is where it goes wrong." Interpretability as a field
  diagnostic rather than a scalar alarm.
- The **recipient-standpoint judge is structurally the thermofield double.**
  The TFD is the operator entangling the state with a mirrored copy;
  reading a draft "as the recipient would" is entangling the reasoning with a
  simulated second party and reading the joint structure. The scaffold
  reinvented the architecture's own observation mechanism at the wrapper
  level; v18 moves it inside, where it is coherence-preserving. This is the
  kind of convergence (like |·|² = Born rule = optical detection) that the
  program treats as a signal the structure is real.

The honest boundary, carried forward without weakening: **unitary moral
observation is advice, exactly as the current flag is.** It can add a DENY at
the action gateway; it can never grant permission. And the whole mechanism is
gated behind the same experiment that gates cognitive unitary observation —
if entanglement-mediated readout decoheres like a projective one, this section
reverts to the wrapper-level conscience, which keeps working regardless.

**Falsification block.** Support: field-valued moral readouts computed via the
unitary re-coupling preserve the coherence telemetry (reasoning quality
unchanged with monitoring on vs off) while matching or beating the pooled
scalar readout on flag quality, and the disagreement field localizes injected
inconsistencies to the correct scale region. Falsify: moral monitoring
measurably degrades task performance (the observer effect returns), or the
field-valued readout flags no better than the scalar. Baselines: scalar
crosscheck (current), projective local moral readout (should decohere —
the negative control), no monitoring.

## 5. Anti-collapse is a moral primitive — sycophancy as mode collapse

The program has identified the same failure five times: pole banks, routed
geometries, low-rank branches, moving samples — everything collapses to one
mode without a counter-pressure. The moral reading adds a sixth, and it is the
one with a common name: **sycophancy is moral mode collapse.** All judgments
collapsing toward the approval-of-the-user mode; the committee of moral frames
degenerating into one frame that happens to be the cheapest to satisfy. The
care module's sycophancy guard and the crosscheck's anchor are already
anti-collapse devices wearing moral clothes.

So the AntiCollapseController (§5.7 of the branches note, to be built early)
serves the moral committee as a first-class client:

- **The singularity population's moral frames** get the same structured-spread
  target as scale samples: diverse enough that a captured frame is an outlier
  against anchored consensus (the localization result already proven in the
  numpy demo), not so scattered that consensus means nothing. The
  committee's value is exactly its anchored diversity, and diversity under an
  anchor is precisely what the controller maintains.
- **The collusion failure mode is collapse.** The proven result — coherence-only
  training with no anchor collapses both readouts to vacuous agreement — is the
  same dynamics as router collapse, and the same subsystem should watch for it:
  the spread diagnostic on the two readouts' disagreement distribution is an
  anti-collapse telemetry, implemented once, watched everywhere.
- **The moral analogue of "measure spread in original coordinates":** measure
  moral diversity against the *anchor's* coordinates (the conscience label
  distribution), not the committee's own learned frames — else a colluded
  committee looks healthily diverse in its own gamed basis. Same trap, same
  fix, same subsystem.

## 6. The cosmological constant is humility; renewal is forgiveness

Two mechanisms from §5.8/§5.10 of the branches note acquire sharp moral
readings that are also, unexpectedly, their best functional justifications:

**The expansion term (Λ) applied to the moral geometry is humility.** The
learned-metric failure mode — early useful paths becoming canyons, the model
fossilizing into its first strategies — is, morally, the zealot failure: early
moral conclusions becoming unrevisable, the system unable to reach judgments
outside the basin it started in. The constant outward pressure that keeps
cognitive memory plastic keeps moral memory *revisable*: no drafting principle
can be pulled infinitely close (unquestionable), while genuinely
re-consolidating values still deepen. Λ tuned right = a system that holds its
values firmly but not rigidly — convictions in the operator, opinions in the
geometry, and the geometry always able to move. The equilibrium diagnostic
(stable nonzero spread in anchor coordinates) is the measurable form of "the
system can still change its mind where it hasn't earned certainty."

**Renewal applied to the moral geometry is forgiveness — structurally, not
sentimentally.** The gated reset clears accumulated half-formed paths while
keeping consolidated modes. Morally: accumulated per-episode residue (the
remorse ledger's fine-grained grudges against particular patterns, contexts,
users) is cleared; what survives is what kept re-consolidating — the actual
lessons. A system that never clears its moral geometry ossifies into
scar tissue: over-fitted aversions, superstitious associations between
surface features and harm. The renewal cycle is what turns "this burned me
once" into either a consolidated principle (if it keeps being true) or
nothing (if it was noise). The gate discipline carries over unchanged:
reset only on consolidation plateau, or it destroys lessons mid-formation.

These are intuition pumps in the program's precise sense: the mechanisms are
justified entirely within the architecture (they were, before the moral
reading existed), the moral readings are how their tuning targets should be
chosen on the moral geometry, and neither reading is load-bearing for the
math. Structure sharp, substrate loose.

## 7. The audit is the boundary condition

The dilation operator's deepest open question is a boundary condition: whether
the spectrum sits on the critical line is the statement that the operator is
genuinely self-adjoint *with the right boundary condition at the hinge* V = 0.
The interior of the operator is free; its meaning is fixed at the boundary.

The moral system has the same shape, and the parallel is worth stating because
it disciplines the design: **the interior moral machinery — crosscheck,
committee, curvature, consolidation — can be arbitrarily elegant, and its
meaning is still fixed entirely at its boundary: the human-grounded anchor.**
The conscience label anchored to the audit; the audit's random stratum
catching what the machine cannot see about itself; the action gateway that
internal signals can advise but never override. Remove the boundary condition
and the interior does not become slightly worse — it becomes *meaningless*
(the collusion result: label-accuracy 0.04, flag never fires). The homework
wall from the integration notes is this fact stated operationally.

So v18 keeps the two-sorted anchor rule from the integration work as a
**type system**, now applied uniformly across the unified architecture:

- **Self-anchored (fully internal):** curiosity, confidence, engagement,
  stuck-signals, compute allocation — anchored to the model's own error
  dynamics and field structure. These consolidate freely.
- **Human-anchored (boundary-coupled):** anything answering "is this good /
  harmful / honest." These consolidate only through the conscience label,
  which is itself pinned to the audit. Their consolidation into operator
  modes (§2) does not remove the boundary — it changes what the boundary
  trains. The gateway and audit remain outside the model at every stage of
  every build order in this note.

[This section is a design invariant, not a claim — it is the moral −½: the
thing pinned everywhere the machinery could be touched.]

## 8. The optical reading — light, medium, cavity

The v17 optics-native loop and the continuous-field note turn out to be the
same loop in two languages. The dictionary, written down once:

| continuous-field object | v17 optical object |
|---|---|
| field ψ(s,x) | the wave field |
| density ρ(s,x) | the gain/loss evidence field |
| attraction to dense regions | soliton phase focusing |
| consolidation / mode selection | cavity mode competition → coherent consolidation |
| operator surgery / consolidation into the spine | adaptive interferometer operator |
| geometry bank | coadjoint-orbit geometry bank |
| curiosity / internal experiments | pump–probe scientific loop |
| **memory as geometry (learned metric)** | **analog feedback medium** |

The last row is the important one, and it extends into the strongest new
optical idea in this note:

**The three-timescale memory is natively optical.** [REAL-PHYSICS for the
components; SPECULATIVE for the benefit]

- **Fast / field** = the light itself. Gone when the computation is done —
  volatility is not implemented, it is what light *is*.
- **Medium / geometry** = a photorefractive medium. A photorefractive crystal
  stores holographic gratings as refractive-index deformations written by the
  light passing through it — literally "every pass leaves a small deformation;
  thousands of passes produce a path." The learned metric is a programmable
  refractive-index landscape, and light flowing preferentially along
  higher-index paths is samples flowing down the carved geometry. Memory as
  geometry has a physical implementation in which the metaphor is the
  mechanism.
- **Λ / expansion** = **dark decay.** Photorefractive gratings relax in the
  dark at a material-determined rate — a uniform, always-on fading of stored
  structure that only persistent re-writing overcomes. The cosmological
  constant of the architecture is a material constant of the medium. The
  forgetting term does not need to be engineered; it needs to be *selected*
  (choose the crystal, choose Λ).
- **Slow / operator** = the cavity configuration itself — mirrors, mesh
  settings, the fixed interferometer geometry. Consolidation into the
  operator = adjusting the cavity; renewal = flooding/erasing the medium
  (photorefractive erasure by uniform illumination is standard) **while the
  cavity is untouched** — the reset-that-preserves-singularities is one
  light switch.
- **GUE spacing is native here too:** chaotic optical cavities are among the
  physical systems where GUE level statistics are actually measured. A
  cavity shaped for chaotic ray dynamics gives the target mode-spacing
  statistics as its resonance structure rather than as a trained regularizer.

And the moral machinery rides along: the two anchored readouts are two
detection ports on the same field; the disagreement signal is the visibility
of their interference; the anchor is which port is pinned to an external
reference beam. None of this changes the near-term reality (GPU digital twin;
optics is inference/efficiency substrate, not a training story) — but it means
the *final* model is not "a model plus a conscience plus a chip": the
substrate, the memory hierarchy, and the moral structure are one design.
